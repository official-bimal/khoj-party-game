# Content guidelines

Use recognizable, specific playable terms with respectful Nepali spellings and Roman variants. Include the Terai, hills and mountains, multiple languages and regional traditions. Keep family defaults. Mark alcohol and mature material with age ≥18; default games exclude these. Avoid invasive facts about people, unsupported allegations, slurs and stereotyping. Category labels are not sources.

A new entry starts draft, including imports. Record a source or explain authored origin; never fabricate provenance. Check canonical, Unicode, aliases, translations, difficulty, hints, suitability, related-word fairness and regional context. Hints must not contain the secret word. Editorial approval requires an explicit named reviewer in the protected workspace; this is an operator assertion, not proof of independent review. Publishing creates a snapshot. Editing an approved word returns it to draft.

Bulk approval is deliberately absent. At least two culturally knowledgeable editors should review sensitive categories before launch. Seed records carry no human approval. A normalized ContentReport table exists; public report submission and review queue are future work. Until then operators can identify problems by exported entry ID and edit/reject through `/admin`.
